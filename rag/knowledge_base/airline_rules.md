# Airline Travel Rules

## Baggage Allowance

Carry-on baggage:

- One carry-on bag is allowed.
- Maximum weight is typically 7 kg.
- Personal items such as laptops or handbags are permitted if they fit under the seat.

Checked baggage:

- Allowance depends on the ticket type and airline.
- Extra baggage fees may apply for overweight or oversized luggage.

---

## Check-in Requirements

Passengers should:

- Complete online check-in when available.
- Arrive at the airport at least:
  - 2 hours before domestic flights.
  - 3 hours before international flights.

Late arrival may result in denied boarding.

---

## Boarding Rules

Passengers must present:

- Valid passport or national ID (where accepted).
- Boarding pass.
- Required visas for international travel.

Boarding gates usually close 15–20 minutes before departure.

---

## Flight Delays

If a flight is delayed, passengers may receive updates through:

- Email
- SMS
- Mobile application notifications

Compensation depends on airline policy and local aviation regulations.

---

## Flight Changes

Passengers may request:

- Date changes
- Time changes
- Destination changes (where permitted)

Additional fare differences and change fees may apply.

---

## Name Corrections

Minor spelling corrections may be allowed.

Changing the passenger to a different person is generally not permitted after ticket issuance.

---

## Special Assistance

Passengers requiring assistance should notify the airline at least 48 hours before departure.

Services may include:

- Wheelchair assistance
- Priority boarding
- Assistance for visually or hearing-impaired travelers

---

## Restricted Items

The following items are generally prohibited:

- Explosives
- Flammable liquids
- Weapons
- Dangerous chemicals

Security regulations vary by country and airport.

---

## Travel Documents

Passengers are responsible for ensuring they have:

- Valid passport
- Required visas
- Health certificates when applicable

Failure to meet entry requirements may result in denied boarding or denied entry.

---

## Missed Flights

If a passenger misses a flight due to personal reasons:

- Refund eligibility depends on the fare rules.
- Rebooking fees may apply.
- Some promotional tickets cannot be changed or refunded.
