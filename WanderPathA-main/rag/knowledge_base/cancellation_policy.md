# Cancellation Policy

## General Policy

Customers may cancel their bookings through the WanderPath platform or by contacting customer support. Cancellation eligibility and refund amounts depend on the cancellation time and the airline or travel provider's rules.

---

## Flight Cancellation

- Cancellations made within 24 hours of booking are eligible for a full refund if the flight departure is at least 7 days away.
- After the 24-hour grace period, airline cancellation rules apply.
- Some promotional or discounted tickets are non-refundable.
- Taxes and government fees may be refundable even when the ticket fare is not.

---

## Hotel Cancellation

- Hotels with free cancellation can be canceled before the deadline shown during booking.
- If the cancellation deadline has passed, one or more nights may be charged depending on the hotel's policy.
- Non-refundable hotel reservations cannot be refunded.

---

## Customer-Initiated Cancellation

Customers who cancel voluntarily may receive:

- Full refund
- Partial refund
- Travel credit
- No refund

The outcome depends on the supplier's cancellation policy.

---

## Airline-Initiated Cancellation

If an airline cancels a flight, customers may choose one of the following options:

- Full refund
- Flight rebooking
- Travel voucher (if offered by the airline)

The available options depend on the airline.

---

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days.
Banks or payment providers may require additional processing time.

---

## Non-Refundable Services

The following services are generally non-refundable:

- Travel insurance (after activation)
- Visa application fees
- Airport transfer services that have already been provided

---

## Exceptional Circumstances

Refund exceptions may apply in cases such as:

- Medical emergencies
- Natural disasters
- Government travel restrictions

Supporting documents may be required.
